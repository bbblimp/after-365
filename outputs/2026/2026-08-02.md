## 1. EU AI Act GPAI rules move from obligation to enforcement

**Original date:** 2025-08-02
**Type:** AI Governance / Digital Regulation / European Union
**Confidence:** High

### Then
On 2025-08-02, the EU AI Act's obligations for providers of general-purpose AI models entered into application. New models placed on the EU market had to meet transparency, documentation, copyright-policy, training-summary, and systemic-risk notification duties, while models already on the market received a later compliance deadline.

### State after 365 days
One year later, the date has become the enforcement threshold for the EU's AI governance system. The Commission's enforcement powers over general-purpose AI obligations begin on 2026-08-02, and the wider AI Act timeline also brings transparency duties for AI-generated or AI-interacting systems into application. The AI Office says its first year was used for technical compliance dialogue, especially with providers following the voluntary Code of Practice.

### Direct impacts
The immediate impact was to turn the AI Act from a framework statute into an active compliance program for foundation-model providers. Providers placing new models on the EU market had to prepare technical files, disclose training-content summaries, respect EU copyright rules, and notify systemic-risk models.

### Indirect impacts
The wider effect was to make the EU a reference jurisdiction for model documentation and downstream-provider information. It also created a live conflict between regulatory certainty, copyright enforcement, open-source exemptions, and claims from technology firms that implementation guidance remained late or unclear.

### What original coverage missed
Initial coverage could mark the legal start date, but it could not yet show whether the first year would become a cooperative compliance period rather than an immediate penalty-driven regime. It also could not show how the 2026 transparency phase would turn the Act toward consumer-facing labels and enforcement staffing.

### One-year assessment
The 2025 start date was consequential because it created the first durable compliance layer for general-purpose AI in a major market. Its significance after 365 days lies less in fines already imposed than in the new enforceable baseline for model transparency, copyright process, and systemic-risk supervision.

## 2. Hasina's tribunal hearings become an extradition test

**Original date:** 2025-08-02
**Type:** Transitional Justice / Political Accountability / Bangladesh
**Confidence:** Medium

### Then
On 2025-08-02, Bangladesh's state news agency reported that prosecutors would open their case the next day against deposed former prime minister Sheikh Hasina, former home minister Asaduzzaman Khan Kamal, and former police chief Chowdhury Abdullah Al-Mamun over crimes against humanity during the 2024 mass uprising. Hasina and Kamal were outside Bangladesh and were to be tried in absentia, while Al-Mamun had been accepted as a state witness.

### State after 365 days
After 365 days, the trial has become a defining accountability and legitimacy case for Bangladesh's post-Hasina order. The tribunal convicted Hasina in absentia in November 2025 and sentenced her to death over the crackdown. By July 2026, India said Bangladesh's extradition request was under legal examination, leaving the legal judgment unresolved as a custody and bilateral-diplomacy question.

### Direct impacts
The hearing schedule moved the case from indictment into public testimony, including live broadcast proceedings and victim accounts. It placed the 2024 uprising's death toll, command responsibility, and state violence into a formal judicial record.

### Indirect impacts
The case also reshaped Bangladesh-India politics. Hasina's continued presence in India, Bangladesh's demand for extradition, and Hasina's rejection of the tribunal as politically driven have made the verdict part of both transitional justice and contested democratic legitimacy.

### What original coverage missed
On the eve of testimony, coverage could identify the charges and the unusual in-absentia posture, but it could not yet know that the case would end in the harshest sentence against a Bangladeshi leader or that extradition would remain open months later.

### One-year assessment
The trial was consequential because it converted the uprising's violence from protest memory into a state-backed judicial claim against the former head of government. Confidence is medium because the verdict and extradition status are clear, while the fairness and political meaning of the tribunal remain disputed by Hasina and her supporters.

## 3. Italy's West Nile deaths become Europe's mosquito-disease warning

**Original date:** 2025-08-02
**Type:** Public Health / Vector-Borne Disease / Europe
**Confidence:** High

### Then
On 2025-08-02, European coverage reported that Italy had recorded ten West Nile virus deaths since the start of the year, including a death in Lazio. The report came during the summer transmission season, when local mosquito-borne infections are typically detected and when travel, heat, and surveillance pressures converge.

### State after 365 days
By the close of the 2025 season, ECDC and EFSA recorded 1,112 locally acquired human West Nile virus cases and 97 deaths in Europe. Italy accounted for 779 cases and 72 deaths, its highest reported annual number of human West Nile cases, while France reported more cases than in any previous year. Early 2026 reporting still described the season as in its initial phase, but ECDC had already framed the 2025 pattern as part of a broader new normal for mosquito-borne disease in Europe.

### Direct impacts
The immediate significance was clinical and operational: deaths in Italy signaled active transmission and the need for mosquito control, blood-safety precautions, clinician awareness, and public protective measures during peak summer risk.

### Indirect impacts
The broader consequence was to link a familiar seasonal disease to changing European risk geography. ECDC connected longer and more intense transmission seasons to warmer temperatures, longer summers, milder winters, and rainfall shifts that create more favorable conditions for mosquito vectors.

### What original coverage missed
Early August coverage could identify a serious Italian cluster, but it could not yet show that Italy would end the year with a national record for reported human West Nile cases or that France would also set a case record.

### One-year assessment
The August deaths mattered because they were an early marker of a season that later became a European public-health warning. The one-year finding is not that West Nile was new, but that surveillance data increasingly treats it as a recurring adaptation problem for temperate Europe.

## 4. The Kilis-Aleppo gas flow becomes a reconstruction-grid test

**Original date:** 2025-08-02
**Type:** Energy Infrastructure / Reconstruction / Syria
**Confidence:** Medium

### Then
On 2025-08-02, Turkey, Azerbaijan, Qatar, and Syria inaugurated the restored Kilis-Aleppo natural gas link and began sending Azerbaijani gas to Syria through Turkey. Officials presented the project as a way to supply the Aleppo power plant, raise electricity availability, and support Syria's post-conflict reconstruction after the fall of the Assad government.

### State after 365 days
After 365 days, the pipeline reads as one concrete piece of a wider but still incomplete regional energy reconstruction effort. Turkish and Qatari officials continued to discuss power-plant investment in 2026, while reporting on Syria's energy sector described persistent rationing, high losses, and uneven improvement despite new fuel and electricity initiatives. Some later accounts said regular pumping and related regional gas arrangements continued into 2026, but independent evidence of broad electricity recovery remains limited.

### Direct impacts
The direct effect was to reopen a cross-border gas route with a stated daily capacity of up to 6 million cubic meters and to connect Syrian electricity recovery to Azerbaijani supply, Turkish transit, and Qatari financing.

### Indirect impacts
The wider consequence was geopolitical as much as technical. The project made Turkey, Qatar, and Azerbaijan visible participants in Syria's reconstruction while reducing the relative role of the old Iran-linked energy pattern and tying return-of-refugees rhetoric to physical infrastructure.

### What original coverage missed
Opening-day coverage could report the ceremony, capacity, and expectations for more hours of power, but it could not yet show whether fuel flows would translate into durable grid recovery across Syria or whether the investment pledges would outpace technical losses and damaged infrastructure.

### One-year assessment
The gas link was consequential because it turned regional support for post-Assad Syria into a physical energy corridor. The restrained conclusion after one year is mixed: the corridor strengthened reconstruction diplomacy, but the evidence does not yet support a finding that Syria's electricity crisis has been solved.
