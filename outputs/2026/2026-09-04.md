## 1. The Bulape Ebola outbreak becomes a containment benchmark with limits

**Original date:** 2025-09-04
**Type:** Public Health / Epidemic Response / Rural Health Systems
**Confidence:** High

### Then
On 2025-09-04, the Democratic Republic of the Congo declared an Ebola virus disease outbreak in Bulape health zone, Kasai Province, after national laboratory testing confirmed Ebola virus in samples from suspected cases. WHO reported 28 suspected cases and 15 deaths as of that date, including deaths among health-care workers.

### State after 365 days
The 2025 Kasai outbreak was declared over on 2025-12-01 after 42 days without a new confirmed case following the last patient's discharge. The final record was 64 cases, 53 confirmed and 11 probable, with 45 deaths. By the one-year mark, however, DRC was confronting a separate 2026 Bundibugyo-virus outbreak and WHO had again pointed back to the 2025 event as the country's immediately preceding Ebola episode.

### Direct impacts
The outbreak killed 45 people, affected six health areas in Bulape health zone, infected five health workers, and required contact tracing, laboratory confirmation, case management, infection-prevention measures, safe burials, and risk communication in a hard-to-reach rural area.

### Indirect impacts
Its containment showed that a small rural Ebola outbreak could still be stopped with coordinated national and international response, but it also exposed persistent vulnerabilities: delayed recognition, nosocomial spread, unsafe burial practices, limited infrastructure, and the continuing risk of new zoonotic spillovers.

### What original coverage missed
Initial coverage could identify suspected cases, deaths, and national risk, but it could not yet show whether Bulape would become a contained outbreak or an amplifier. Later reporting narrowed the record to a localized, high-fatality event that ended, while the 2026 emergency showed that successful containment did not remove DRC's broader Ebola risk.

### One-year assessment
The one-year legacy is mixed. The Bulape response became a credible containment case, not a runaway epidemic, but its lessons were not a durable shield against renewed Ebola pressure. The event is best read as evidence of response capacity under strain rather than evidence that the underlying health-security problem had been solved.

## 2. Nepal's platform ban becomes a government-toppling trigger

**Original date:** 2025-09-04
**Type:** Digital Rights / Protest Politics / Democratic Transition
**Confidence:** High

### Then
On 2025-09-04, Nepal ordered internet service providers to block major unregistered social media platforms, including Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, X, WhatsApp, Reddit, and LinkedIn, after the companies failed to meet local registration requirements. The government framed the move as regulatory compliance; media and civil-society groups warned that it restricted press freedom and access to information.

### State after 365 days
Within days, the ban became one of the triggers for youth-led Gen Z protests over corruption and governance. Human Rights Watch later recorded that police used excessive and lethal force on 2025-09-08, killing protesters outside parliament and in Itahari, and that the unrest contributed to Prime Minister K.P. Oli's fall and the appointment of an interim government under former Chief Justice Sushila Karki. A 2026 UK country note recorded a larger official death toll for the September unrest, the dissolution of parliament, a judicial inquiry, and later arrests tied to the response.

### Direct impacts
The immediate effect was disruption of communications, journalism, business, tourism, and family contact through widely used platforms. The political effect arrived quickly: the ban helped focus youth anger into demonstrations that security forces met with lethal force.

### Indirect impacts
The decision turned platform-registration policy into a constitutional-stability issue. It shifted debate from company compliance to censorship, accountability for protest deaths, and whether digital governance can be used as a pretext for suppressing lawful expression.

### What original coverage missed
First-day coverage captured the breadth of the block and objections from press-freedom groups. It could not yet show that the social-media order would become part of the causal chain in a government collapse, a judicial inquiry, and a broader reset of Nepal's political timetable.

### One-year assessment
The ban's significance was not technical enforcement against foreign platforms. It became a trigger event showing how digital-rights controls can interact with corruption grievances and weak public trust. The one-year record makes the 2025-09-04 order a marker in Nepal's political crisis rather than a routine communications regulation.

## 3. The flood-control protest becomes an infrastructure-corruption case

**Original date:** 2025-09-04
**Type:** Infrastructure Governance / Anti-Corruption / Disaster Risk
**Confidence:** High

### Then
On 2025-09-04, flood survivors and activist groups protested outside property linked to contractor Sarah Discaya amid Philippine investigations into allegedly anomalous flood-control projects. Malacanang urged the public to avoid violence and allow due process, while the Presidential Communications Office said public works officials connected to questioned Bulacan projects were being removed, referred for charges, or placed under immigration lookout.

### State after 365 days
A year later, the street protest had become part of a larger accountability record. The scandal led to an Independent Commission for Infrastructure, criminal filings, asset freezes, arrests, and court proceedings. By June 2026, a Bulacan court had ordered the arrest of Pacifico 'Curlee' Discaya, Cezarah 'Sarah' Discaya, and others over alleged malversation connected to a flood-control project; by July 2026, Philippine reporting said recovered kickback money remitted to the treasury had reached PHP841 million.

### Direct impacts
The September protest helped move public anger from hearings and disclosures into visible pressure on contractors and the public works system. It also coincided with personnel removals, lookout bulletins, blacklisting steps, and preparation of complaints against officials and contractors.

### Indirect impacts
The broader effect was to connect corruption control with disaster governance. Flood-control projects were no longer only budget or procurement line items; they became evidence in a public argument about why communities remained vulnerable to flooding despite large infrastructure spending.

### What original coverage missed
Original coverage could show anger at the Discaya-linked firms and the administration's promise of due process. It could not yet measure whether the scandal would reach courts, recover funds, or implicate higher-level political actors rather than stopping at contractors and district engineers.

### One-year assessment
The one-year record shows partial institutional movement rather than closure. The case produced arrests, charges, and restitution claims, but allegations of selective accountability persisted. Its lasting significance lies in turning flood infrastructure into a governance test: whether a climate-vulnerable state can prove that public-works spending translates into protection rather than patronage.

## 4. Armani's death becomes a controlled sale plan

**Original date:** 2025-09-04
**Type:** Corporate Succession / Luxury Industry / Cultural Economy
**Confidence:** High

### Then
On 2025-09-04, Giorgio Armani died in Milan at 91. Reuters described him as the founder and driving force of an independent fashion group with about EUR2.3 billion in annual turnover, and noted that his death raised immediate questions about succession at a house he had personally controlled for five decades.

### State after 365 days
The follow-up record quickly changed the succession story. Armani's will instructed heirs to sell an initial 15 percent stake within 18 months, giving priority to LVMH, L'Oreal, EssilorLuxottica, or a comparable luxury-sector buyer, followed by a larger sale or a listing within three to five years. Reuters later reported that Pantaleo Dell'Orco, Armani's longtime partner and close collaborator, would chair the foundation central to the future sale plan.

### Direct impacts
The founder's death ended a highly personal governance model and placed family members, Dell'Orco, senior managers, and the Giorgio Armani Foundation into formal control roles. It also put a previously guarded independent luxury company into a defined process for partial sale, eventual majority transfer, or market listing.

### Indirect impacts
The will created a potential acquisition path for Europe's largest luxury and beauty groups and made Armani a test of whether founder culture can be preserved through voting rights, foundation vetoes, and phased ownership change. It also weakened assumptions that the company would simply remain independent after the founder's death.

### What original coverage missed
Obituaries correctly emphasized Armani's creative influence and the absence of an obvious single successor. What they could not yet show was the will's sharper instruction: not only continuity under trusted insiders, but a legally structured route toward sale or public listing.

### One-year assessment
Armani's death became a corporate-governance event as much as a cultural loss. The brand's creative legacy remained central, but the durable consequence was the conversion of one of Italian fashion's most independent founder-led houses into a managed succession and sale process.
